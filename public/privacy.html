Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Drill Finder is a coaching drill library and practice-planning tool. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We have tried to keep it short and readable.

Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Your email address and password. Required to create an account so your favorites, saved drills, and practice plans follow you between devices. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never visible to us.
  • Content you create. Drills you add, drills you mark as favorites, and practice plans you build are stored with your account.
  • Subscription status. Whether your account has an active Premium subscription, and the identifier that links your account to your payment record.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card numbers and billing details are entered on Stripe's systems and are never sent to or stored by Drill Finder. We receive only your subscription status and a customer reference identifier. Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

How your information is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers in the United States. Access is restricted so that each account can read and write only its own records. The app is hosted on Vercel and served over an encrypted connection.

How we use your information

Your information is used to sign you in, save your work, and determine whether you have access to Premium features. We may email you about your account, such as password resets or billing notices. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days. If you have an active subscription, cancel it before requesting deletion, or let us know and we will cancel it for you.

Children's privacy

Drill Finder is intended for coaches, instructors, and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how we handle your information, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Drill Finder after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to gakorolev@gmail.com.
