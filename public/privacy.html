Drill Finder Privacy Policy Last updated: October 12, 2026 This policy explains what information Drill Finder (the website at drill-finder.vercel.app and the Drill Finder Android app) collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. Drill Finder is a tool that helps sports coaches find practice drills. Using Drill Finder without an account You can search drills, save favorites, build practice plans and add your own drills without signing in. That information is stored only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser or app data. Information we collect if you create an account Email address and password. Used only to sign you in. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form by our sign-in provider; we cannot see them. Premium status. Whether your account has an active Drill Finder Premium subscription. Payments Premium subscriptions are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, you enter your payment details directly with Stripe; Drill Finder never receives or stores your card number. Stripe tells us that a payment succeeded and which account it belongs to, so we can turn on Premium. Stripe's own privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy. How we use information We use your information only to run Drill Finder: to sign you in, to unlock Premium features you paid for, and to reply if you contact us. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Who we share information with We share information only with the service providers that make the app work: Supabase, which stores account sign-in data and Premium status. Stripe, which processes payments. Vercel, which hosts the website and, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and reliability. We may also disclose information if required by law. Children Drill Finder is intended for coaches, teachers and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Keeping or deleting your data We keep your account information while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing us at the address below. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription automatically; you can cancel through Stripe's receipt email or by contacting us. Security Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored with trusted providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
